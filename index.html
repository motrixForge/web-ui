What we're building

Block Craft

In development

Drop blocks. Clear lines. Don't get stuck.

Drag pieces onto an 8×8 board and clear them by filling a whole row or column. No timer — the only pressure is the space you have left, and the pieces you're handed next.

The studio

Small, and honest about it.

An independent studio at the very start of its story. No back catalogue, no publisher — just a first game in development and a clear idea of the kind of games we want to make.

We care about the moment a player stops tapping and starts thinking.

  • Rules first

    A game ships only when its core rule is explainable in one sentence.

  • Respect the player

    Your time and attention matter more than any metric we could chase.

  • Hand-tuned

    Tools help us draft levels. A human signs off on every one that ships.

  • Finish properly

    One title at a time, supported after launch instead of abandoned.

How we work

From spark to shipped

The path Block Craft is walking right now.

  1. 01

    Spark

    One mechanic, prototyped small. If it isn't fun grey-boxed, it never will be.

  2. 02

    Forge

    Stress the rule until it produces puzzles we can't solve on the first try.

  3. 03

    Temper

    Playtest with real people. Reshape the difficulty curve instead of padding it.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Release when it's ready, then keep improving it in the open.
