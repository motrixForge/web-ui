About Block Craft Get in touch

The studio

Games that give people a good few minutes.

We're a small independent studio at the very start of its story — one game in development and a simple goal: make puzzles that people genuinely enjoy, on a bus, in a queue, or before bed.

We'd love for our games to reach a lot of players. Not to chase numbers, but because a game is only really finished when someone out there is having fun with it.

  • Rules first

    A game ships only when its core rule is explainable in one sentence.

  • Respect the player

    Your time and attention matter more than any metric we could chase.

  • Hand-tuned

    Tools help us draft levels. A human signs off on every one that ships.

  • Finish properly

    One title at a time, supported after launch instead of abandoned.

What we're building

Block Craft

In development

Drop blocks. Clear lines. Don't get stuck.

Drag pieces onto an 8×8 board and clear them by filling a whole row or column. No timer — the only pressure is the space you have left, and the pieces you're handed next.

Still in development, no release date yet. Want to hear when it's playable?
